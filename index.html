Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

Tenara respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

When you join the Tenara waitlist, we collect your email address so we can notify you about product updates and launch information.

Tenara uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage information such as app usage events and device information to help us understand app performance and improve the user experience. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Data Storage

Information you enter into Tenara, such as tenant information and rent payments, is currently stored locally on your device. Tenara does not currently upload this information to our servers.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Notify users about Tenara updates and availability
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Provide support

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted service providers, such as analytics providers, to help operate and improve Tenara.

Delete Your Data

To request deletion of data associated with your use of Tenara, email angelalehru@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Data" from the email address you used, or describe your request. We will locate and delete the matching record within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

What this deletes: The email address you gave us if you joined the waitlist using the "Notify Me" feature.

What this doesn't affect: Tenant information and payment records you enter into Tenara which are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting a tenant in the app, or uninstalling Tenara removes that data immediately. Anonymous Firebase Analytics events (see "Information We Collect" above) aren't linked to your identity, so they can't be matched to an individual deletion request.

Children's Privacy

Tenara is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the effective date above.
