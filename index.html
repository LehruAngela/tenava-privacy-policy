Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

Tenava helps landlords record tenants and rent payments, and share that work with people they trust. This Privacy Policy explains what information Tenava collects, how it's used, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Information We Collect

Your account. When you sign in with Google or email, we receive your name and your email address. Email and password sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password.

Rental records you enter. Tenant names, rent amounts, move-in and due dates, payments, rent history, and your preferred currency.

Collaboration. The email addresses of people you invite, and the names and email addresses of the people in your workspace.

Subscriptions. If you subscribe to Tenava Pro, Google Play processes the payment. We receive only your subscription status (for example, which plan and when it renews or ends) through our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We never receive your card or payment details.

Usage information. Firebase Analytics collects app usage events and device information (such as device model and operating system) to help us understand performance and improve the app.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and rental records are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), on Google Cloud servers. A copy is also kept on your phone so the app works offline; it syncs when you're back online.

If you used Tenava before accounts were introduced, the records saved on your phone are copied into your account the first time you sign in.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Store your records, sync them across devices, and calculate balances and due dates
  • Let you work with the people you invite to your workspace
  • Provide Tenava Pro to subscribers
  • Send rent reminders on your phone, if you turn them on
  • Respond to support requests
  • Understand how the app is used, fix problems and improve it

Who Can See Your Data

People in your workspace. Anyone you invite can see your workspace's tenants and payments. Editors can also change them; Viewers can only look. You can change their access or remove them at any time.

Service providers. We use Google (Firebase and Google Play) and RevenueCat to run Tenava. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms: Google and RevenueCat.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Cloud. Access rules ensure only members of a workspace can read its data, and only owners and Editors can change it.

How Long We Keep Data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our systems; copies in our routine backups are removed automatically within 30 days.

Delete Your Data

You can delete your account and data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. If you own a workspace, this deletes it with all its tenants, payments and history, and anyone you invited loses access. If you only help with someone else's workspace, you're removed from it and their records stay with them.

If you can't use the app, see how to request account deletion or email tenava.support@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Tenava is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the effective date above.
