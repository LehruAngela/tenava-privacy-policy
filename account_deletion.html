Delete Your Account

You can delete your Tenava account and its data at any time, either in the app or by emailing us.

In the app

  1. Open Tenava and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in more than a few minutes ago, Tenava will ask you to sign out and sign back in first, to make sure it's really you.

By email

If you can't use the app, email tenava.support@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Tenava account”, from the email address you use to sign in to Tenava. We'll delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • If you own a workspace: the workspace and everything in it, including tenants, payments, rent history and pending invites. Anyone you invited loses access to it.
  • If you help manage someone else's workspace: you're removed from it. The owner's tenants and payments stay with them, including any you recorded.

What we keep

Nothing linked to you. Copies in our routine backups are removed automatically within 30 days. Anonymous usage statistics from Firebase Analytics aren't linked to your identity, so there's nothing there to delete.
